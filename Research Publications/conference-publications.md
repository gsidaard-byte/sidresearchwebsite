## Overview

The CV lists more than 80 peer-reviewed conference papers spanning:

- wingtip vortex and wake physics
- aerodynamic efficiency and flow control
- propeller ground, ceiling, and tandem effects
- gust encounters and closed-loop control
- wing-wing interaction and distributed lift
- event-camera and velocimetry methods
- morphing wings
- agricultural spray drift and nozzle characterization
