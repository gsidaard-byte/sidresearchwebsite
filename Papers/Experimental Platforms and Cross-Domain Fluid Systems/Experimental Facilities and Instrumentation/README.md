# Experimental Facilities and Instrumentation

Parent category: **Experimental Platforms and Cross-Domain Fluid Systems**

## Important Findings

These papers treat the tunnel, jet, and measurement environment as part of the scientific result rather than background plumbing. Platform fidelity, frequency response, and manufacturability all shape what later aerodynamics can be trusted.

## Value

Their value is foundational: once the facility is characterized, every later result gains a more credible experimental base.

## Key Takeaways

Building and validating the instrumented environment is itself a fluid-dynamics research contribution, not just setup work.

## Papers

- **Frequency Response of a Shuttered Open Jet Wind Tunnel** (`cook-et-al-2020-frequency-response-of-a-shuttered-open-jet-wind-tunnel.pdf`)
  Primarily characterizes the experimental platform used to create unsteady flow inputs. The contribution is a more trustworthy wind-jet base for later experiments.
- **Design and Analysis of an Additive Manufactured Supersonic Wind Tunnel** (`gazella-et-al-2020-design-and-analysis-of-an-additive-manufactured-supersonic-wind-tunnel.pdf`)
  This paper demonstrates how additive manufacturing can enable a capable supersonic test section. Its value is in making the facility itself part of the research result.
