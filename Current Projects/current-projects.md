# Current Projects

Source: extracted from `Sid_Most Recent_CV (Jan 2026).pdf`

## Active Research Themes

### AI in Education

- integration of AI into engineering education
- AI-enhanced learning experiences for undergraduate students
- faculty workshops on practical and ethical AI use in higher education
- entrepreneurial mindset and AI in teaching practice
- curriculum development around AI literacy and classroom adoption

### Propeller Aerodynamics and Distributed Propulsion

- tandem propeller performance with vertical offset
- variable-pitch propellers in forward or near-edgewise flight
- wing-embedded propulsor systems
- propeller ground and ceiling effects
- gust response of powered wings and propellers

### Morphing Wings and Lift Distribution

- morphing wings with spanwise twist
- skin-actuated morphing wing concepts
- non-elliptical and bell-shaped lift distributions
- wake characterization of tailored spanwise loading

### Agricultural Spray and Drone Applications

- agricultural nozzle spray geometry in propeller wake
- rotary atomizer spray geometry under propeller influence
- spray drift and precision-agriculture testing

### Flow Diagnostics and Event-Based Sensing

- event-based velocimetry
- focused-ultrasound-induced acoustic streaming
- stereo event-based velocimetry for 3D flowfields
