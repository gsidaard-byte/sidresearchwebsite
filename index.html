Aerodynamics, Propulsion, Diagnostics, Education

Advancing Aerodynamics Research, with Students at the Center

Our research group is built around hands-on student discovery. Students take part in work spanning propeller aerodynamics, morphing wings, wake physics, event-based flow diagnostics, drone applications, and AI in engineering education. Across undergraduate and graduate projects, they design experiments, perform hands-on work in the lab, analyze data, and contribute to scholarly work. The result is research that advances the field while helping students grow into confident engineers and scholars.
