Effect of Propeller Incidence Angle on Wing Embedded Propeller Configuration in Forward Flight

Citation: 49. Gogidze, George J., Sidaard Gunasekaran, and Jielong Cai. "Effect of Propeller Incidence Angle on Wing Embedded Propeller Configuration in Forward Flight." In AIAA SCITECH 2023 Forum, p. 2284. 2023. https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2023-2284

Short abstract: We continue our study on the effect of a propeller embedded in the wing under various propeller incidence angles. The current study focuses on the performance of the wing and the wing-embedded propulsion as one system.
