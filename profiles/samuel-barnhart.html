Investigation of Doppler Lidar for Velocity Measurements in Wind Tunnels

Citation: 24. Barnhart, Samuel A., and Sidaard Gunasekaran. "Investigation of Doppler Lidar for Velocity Measurements in Wind Tunnels." In AIAA Scitech 2020 Forum, p. 1021. 2020. https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2020-1021

Short abstract: A low-cost single component lidar velocimetry technique was designed and tested using a 200-mW continuous wave (CW) laser at the Laser Optical Communication Institute (LOCI), University of Dayton to be used in the wind tunnels for non-intrusive velocity measurement. A numerical framework for a scaled down Lidar system was developed to predict the required laser power and detector sensitivity for multiple velocities and shorter target distances.
