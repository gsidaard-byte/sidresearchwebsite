Overview

Making flow structures visible.

The water tunnel supports experiments and demonstrations where students can directly observe wake development, vortex motion, and flow response around simple and complex models.

Teaching and Research Support

The facility is useful for connecting measured behavior to visual flow physics in classroom and lab settings.
