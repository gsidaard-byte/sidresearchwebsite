## Teaching Approach

Teaching is centered on applied aerodynamics, experimentation, entrepreneurial mindset, and project-based learning. The CV highlights repeated use of:

- passion projects in aerodynamics
- design-build-test experiences in flight education
- flight-simulator-based problem and project learning
- writing-to-learn and case-study based instruction
- AI-supported and entrepreneurial-minded learning experiences
