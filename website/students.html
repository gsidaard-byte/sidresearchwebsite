Current Students

Our Team

Current student work spans undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral projects in aerodynamics, propulsion, diagnostics, spray applications, and engineering education. Every project is student-led. Every publication carries a student name.

Student Community

Undergraduates, master's students, and doctoral researchers contribute side by side — running experiments, presenting at conferences, and building the skills that carry them forward.
