Community

Years of shared work, growth, and community.

This is not just a research group. It is a research community built through more than projects. It grows through lab meetings, conference travel, mentoring, celebrations, and the everyday moments where students learn, work, and support one another.

What Community Looks Like

  • Showing up for each other in the lab and beyond
  • Traveling, presenting, and celebrating together
  • Learning from one another across classes and cohorts
  • Cheering each other on through wins and hard moments
  • Building memories that last beyond graduation
