Research Subcategory

Non-Contact Optical Diagnostics

Flow Diagnostics and Event-Based Sensing / Non-Contact Optical Diagnostics

Important Findings

This subcategory shows how non-contact diagnostics resolve flow fields where intrusive probes or ordinary imaging are inadequate, using Doppler lidar and back-imaged pressure-sensitive paint in challenging geometries.

Value

The value is measurement reach: these techniques let researchers interrogate velocity or pressure signatures while preserving the flow they are trying to study.

Key Takeaways

Alternative optical diagnostics are not just substitutes for PIV; they open access to boundary conditions and geometries that would otherwise be difficult to measure well.
