Research Category

Engineering Education and Entrepreneurial Learning

This category collects papers whose main contribution is pedagogical design, learning structure, and research on how engineering concepts are taught. It creates a dedicated home for educational scholarship and future entrepreneurial-minded learning modules connected to the broader research program. The common thread is capability-building through learning. These papers focus on how students engage with aerodynamics, how instruction can connect equations to real flow behavior, and how teaching models can support stronger engineering reasoning.

Methods and Tools

Course design, experiential learning models, classroom-based assessment, context-rich instruction, and entrepreneurial mindset development.

Why It Matters

Engineering progress depends not only on discoveries, but also on how effectively new engineers learn to reason, design, and create. These papers make education a visible and scholarly part of the research portfolio.
