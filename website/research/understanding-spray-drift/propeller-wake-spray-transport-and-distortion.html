Propeller-Wake Spray Transport and Distortion

Understanding Spray Drift / Propeller-Wake Spray Transport and Distortion

Important Findings

This group shows how an imposed propeller wake reshapes spray geometry downstream, making droplet distributions less predictable and often less uniform than atomizer-only behavior would suggest.

Value

The value is immediately practical for agricultural application, because it shows that rotor-induced flow can dominate drift behavior and can also corrupt measurements if the wake is ignored.

Key Takeaways

Spray performance cannot be evaluated in isolation when a propeller is present; the wake becomes part of the delivery system and must be treated as such.
