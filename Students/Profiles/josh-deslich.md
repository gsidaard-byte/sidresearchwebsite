### 2. Effects of spanloading and slew angle on an Oblique Flying Wing.

- Citation: 26. Deslich, Josh, Sidaard Gunasekaran, Peter Flick, and Douglas Szczublewski. "Effects of spanloading and slew angle on an Oblique Flying Wing." In AIAA Scitech 2020 Forum, p. 0532. 2020. https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2020-0532
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2020-0532
- Short abstract: Designing tail-less aircraft has historically involved vehicle stability challenges, leading to issues further in the design process. Motivated from the work of the Horten brothers and Prandtl, this research aims to take an initial step at exploring an Oblique Flying Wing configuration (OFW) with different geometric twist distributions to understand the impact on the stability of the moment coefficients and aerodynamic performance.
