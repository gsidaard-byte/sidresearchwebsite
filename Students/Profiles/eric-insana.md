## Publications

### 1. Low Reynolds Number Experimental Aerodynamic Verification of Scaled and LEWICE Simulated Ice Accretions in SLD Conditions.

- Citation: 40. Insana, Eric, and Sidaard Gunasekaran. "Low Reynolds Number Experimental Aerodynamic Verification of Scaled and LEWICE Simulated Ice Accretions in SLD Conditions." In AIAA SCITECH 2022 Forum, p. 2025. 2022. https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2022-2025
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2022-2025
- Short abstract: An aerodynamic verification of the recommended scaling method by Anderson and Tsao and the LEWICE software was performed for ice formations generated underneath Duper-cooled Large Drop (SLD) cloud conditions specified under Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR), Part 25, Appendix O envelope. All experiments were performed at the University of Dayton Low Speed Wind Tunnel (UD-LSWT).
